# EcoSense AI – Intelligent Energy Consumption Optimization System

## 1. Project Overview
EcoSense AI is a software-based AI system designed to analyze and optimize energy consumption in buildings and factories.

The system uses energy data and machine learning techniques to identify unnecessary energy usage, predict energy demand, and suggest ways to reduce energy wastage.

## 2. Problem Statement
Buildings and factories often waste electricity because lighting, air conditioning, and other equipment are not managed according to occupancy, weather conditions, or operational requirements.

This leads to unnecessary energy consumption and increased electricity costs.

## 3. Objectives
- Analyze energy consumption patterns.
- Predict future energy demand using machine learning.
- Identify unusual or unnecessary energy usage.
- Recommend energy-saving actions.
- Develop a user-friendly dashboard to display energy information.

## 4. Proposed Solution
We propose a software-based AI energy management system that processes energy datasets, analyzes consumption patterns, predicts energy demand, and provides optimization recommendations.

The initial prototype will use available datasets or simulated data rather than physical IoT hardware.

## 5. Main Features
- Energy consumption analysis
- Energy demand prediction
- Energy wastage detection
- Smart energy-saving recommendations
- Interactive dashboard
- Energy reports and visualizations

## 7. Project Workflow
1. Collect or prepare energy consumption data.
2. Clean and process the dataset.
3. Analyze energy usage patterns.
4. Develop machine learning models.
5. Detect unusual energy consumption.
6. Generate optimization recommendations.
7. Display results through a dashboard.

## 8. Expected Outcomes
